2019

Where it all started

I started without a plan, a roadmap, or even a clear idea of what I was doing. I simply found something that made me want to keep learning. Night after night, I experimented, failed, rebuilt, and questioned everything. Three months later, the answer was clear: this was the path I wanted to follow.

2020

Where Coding Became a Career

Learning on my own felt safe because every mistake was mine to make. Then someone placed their project in my hands, and suddenly every detail carried weight. That first year of client work taught me more than months of practice ever could — not because the projects were difficult, but because someone was trusting me to turn their vision into something real.

2021

Beyond what I knew

The brief looked impossible from the start. Every pixel had to be intentional, every interaction had to work, and the project forced me to learn new techniques while I was already deep into the build. It was easily my biggest challenge at the time. Finishing it didn't just complete a project — it changed the way I think about building.

2022

Leveling up

GSAP evolved from a tool I used occasionally into a core part of how I build websites. As a web developer, I began using animation not just for visual appeal, but to guide users, create smoother interactions, and give each interface its own character. With every project, I focused more on writing thoughtful code, refining interactions, improving performance, and turning ideas into digital experiences that feel purposeful from the first click to the last.

2023

From trust to referrals

Clients I had worked with started returning with new projects, while others began recommending me to people I had never even met. There were no formal pitches or portfolio presentations — just a simple message: "Work with Dipendra, he delivers." For me, that kind of trust goes beyond anything that can be displayed in a case study.

2024

Crafting With Purpose

This was the year I became more intentional about what I built. Instead of following every new trend, I focused on technologies, interactions, and ideas that genuinely improved the project. I experimented less for the sake of experimentation and made more decisions designed to stay valuable over time.

2026

The journey continues

The industry doesn't stand still and neither do I. After five years of working, learning, and evolving, AI has opened up a whole new layer of what's possible. Same obsession, new tools. The best work is still ahead.

Who is Dipendra Xettri?

I’m Dipendra Xettri, a web developer focused on creating modern, interactive, responsive, and visually engaging digital experiences. I enjoy combining clean development with creative UI, animation, and emerging web technologies.

What type of websites do you create?

I create modern websites including personal portfolios, business websites, school and institutional websites, landing pages, interactive experiences, and custom web interfaces.

What technologies do you work with?

My work mainly involves HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, animation, and modern web technologies, with an interest in areas such as Three.js, WebGL, UI/UX, and interactive 3D experiences.

Do you build responsive websites?

Yes. I design and develop websites to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, with particular attention to usability, layout, performance, and touch interactions.

Can you create 3D and interactive websites?

Yes. I’m interested in creating immersive web experiences using technologies such as Three.js and WebGL, including interactive objects, cinematic animations, 3D environments, and scroll-based experiences.

Do you work on existing websites?

Yes. I can work with existing websites to improve their design, responsiveness, animations, performance, user experience, and functionality, depending on the project and codebase.

What is your development process?

My typical process follows: DISCOVER → DEFINE → DESIGN → BUILD → REFINE → DEPLOY. I first understand the purpose and requirements, then develop the experience, refine the details, test responsiveness, and prepare the project for deployment.

Not sure which plan fits your project?
